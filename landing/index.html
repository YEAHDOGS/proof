    0

    cannabis-involved ER visits, US, 2023

    SAMHSA DAWN. “Involving” means cannabis was recorded among the substances, not the sole cause. Receipt

    Cannabis harms show up in ERs, not morgues. The documented non-lethal harms: impairment, dependence, acute anxiety and psychosis episodes. Cannabis-linked psychosis and schizophrenia risk is real science the dataset does not yet quantify; it sits on the project’s open question list, not in its charts.

    Sources, not claims

    The receipts.

    Two kinds of numbers. Reported counts come straight from mortality records and surveys. Modelled estimates attribute deaths across conditions (alcohol, tobacco) and are a different, broader kind of figure. Categories overlap: a death involving fentanyl and cocaine appears in both series, so never sum the rows.

    • SAMHSA NSDUHUse figures. Annual survey, ages 12+.
    • CDC ARDIAlcohol-attributable deaths, modelled.
    • NIDA overdose death ratesOverdose counts from NCHS final data.
    • CDC WONDERUnderlying mortality records.
    • SAMHSA DAWNEmergency department visits.
    • DEA drug fact sheetThe cited zero.
    • 2014 Surgeon General’s reportSmoking-attributable deaths, still cited by CDC.
    • WHO~2.3 billion current drinkers worldwide.
    • Whitney EconomicsTexas hemp retail, 2025. Industry-commissioned.
