How the numbers are made

METHOD

Modelled or reported. Named, never blurred.

Reported

Counts straight from mortality records and surveys. NCHS overdose data, NSDUH, DAWN.

Modelled

Estimates attributing deaths across conditions. CDC ARDI, the 2014 Surgeon General report. Broader, larger, different.

Every figure carries its verification status. Seeded numbers get cross-checked before they flip.
